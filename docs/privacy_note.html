Design rule: collect less, keep less, show everything. The DPDP Rules were notified on 13 Nov 2025; consent-manager rules apply from Nov 2026 and the core Data Fiduciary duties (notice, consent, security, breach reporting, retention, rights) from 13 May 2027. Ek Baithak meets those duties today, so Accel does not need to re-engineer later.

2. Consent that people can actually read

  • Notice shown before KYC, standalone (Rule 3), in the owner's language (10 Indian languages), one line per purpose.
  • Required purposes (KYC, installation) are separated from optional ones (registry fetch, WhatsApp, marketing); nothing optional is pre-ticked except service updates the owner can switch off.
  • Every consent gets a receipt ID + hash, versioned notice, timestamp and language. Format is ready for registered Consent Managers.
  • Withdraw as easily as you gave: one tap in "My data", with the effect explained.

3. Data minimisation by design

  • Registry first, documents last: a GSTIN pre-fills name, address and constitution; Entity Locker replaces the GST and Udyam uploads. Typical owner uploads drop from 4 to 1 (a recent bill), and identity is confirmed by Aadhaar OTP at signing instead of an ID photo.
  • The advisor asks only for what changes the bundle (type, city, size, sites, needs). No date of birth, no PAN, no bank details.
  • Partners see only the installation address, contact name and a masked phone number; their access ends 7 days after installation.
  • Back-office and logs show masked identifiers (27PZ••••••••1ZH); full values only to the KYC role.

4. AI and third parties

  • OCR runs on our own server (RapidOCR); documents are not sent to any AI service to be read.
  • An AI "second reader" sees a document only when a hard check fails (e.g. GSTIN check digit) and is never the sole basis for a decision. Eligibility, price and KYC verdicts are plain code with tests (12/12 specimen applicants correct).
  • Chat AI receives the conversation and catalogue, not documents. The prototype uses Gemini's free tier on synthetic data; production uses a paid, no-training enterprise tier in an Indian region, under a Data Processing Agreement.
  • No data goes to model providers hosted outside approved regions.

5. Security controls

  • TLS in transit; AES-256 at rest (prod); secrets in environment variables, never in code or logs.
  • Role-based access: owner, partner, sales, KYC reviewer, auditor.
  • Tamper-evident audit log: every access, consent, KYC decision and erasure is hash-chained and kept at least one year (Rule 6).
  • Fraud defence: check digit, registry near-match finder (caught an altered GSTIN), two independent readers must agree before flagging tampering, human review for anything flagged.
  • Breach response: contain, inform affected owners and the Data Protection Board without delay, detailed report within 72 hours.

6. Owner rights, built into the app

  • "My data" shows everything held, why and until when; download as a file; correct details; erase (we explain what the telecom licence obliges us to keep); grievance answered well within the 90-day limit.
